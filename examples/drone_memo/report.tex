\begin{abstract}
We evaluate the position-estimation accuracy of a small quadrotor flying a square pattern
in an indoor test volume over a \valu{drone.trajectory.duration_s} flight. Over
\val{drone.loc_err.n_samples} aligned samples, the mean horizontal error relative to the
\val{drone.loc_err.reference} position was \valu{drone.loc_err.mean_cm} (median
\valu{drone.loc_err.median_cm}, 95th percentile \valu{drone.loc_err.p95_cm}), and
\valu{drone.loc_err.pct_under_10cm} of samples fell within \valu{drone.loc_err.threshold_cm}. The beacon system produced
\val{drone.failures.n_events} failure events, with \valu{drone.failures.availability_pct}
availability.
\end{abstract}
